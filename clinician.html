Calibrate the wrist

This tells the system what "relaxed" is for this patient and how far they can comfortably move. Targets are always a percentage of this comfortable range, never beyond it.

B · Relaxed position

Ask the patient to rest the forearm on the support, palm down, and let the hand relax flat. Then press:

C · Comfortable range

Press start, then ask the patient to move gently as far as is comfortable: up, down, toward the thumb, toward the little finger, then (elbow tucked in at the side) palm down and palm up. Press finish.

D · Pain before we start (0 = none, 10 = worst)

The NHS leaflet says to work to an "unpleasant stretch", about 5/10, which should settle within about 30 minutes after the exercises. Stop if anything feels sharp.

Exercise buttons

Camera gestures: make one clear, quick stroke with your right hand (up, down, to your left, to your right) to give a wrist exercise. Touch your left thumb to a fingertip (or to the base of your little finger) and hold it for half a second to give that thumb exercise. Make a left fist for half a second to ask for a full fist. Mozart copies your arms and hands the whole time.

Long holds: when the patient reaches a stretch with a long hold, the orchestra holds a fermata (a long growing chord) and the headset counts down, so the 10-30 seconds feel like part of the music.
