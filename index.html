REHARMONY

A concert for your recovery

I am the Maestro

The clinician. Laptop with a webcam and a speaker. Mozart copies your arms, you choose the exercises.
You'll get a 4-digit code and a QR code to give to the player.

I am the Player

The patient. Quest 3 headset (open this page in the Quest Browser). Type the code from the Maestro's screen.

Both devices just need internet: they don't have to be on the same Wi-Fi. When the website runs from the team laptop (START_SERVER.bat), the headset pairs by itself and the code is optional.
